# Van Racing

A chaos racer combining believable delivery vans and scenery with exaggerated crashes.

## Language

**Van**:
The delivery vehicle a player drives around the track, which can tumble and shed parts during crashes.
_Avoid_: Truck

**Driver ragdoll**:
The floppy, bloodless state of a driver ejected from a van during a severe crash.

**Collision target**:
An unoccupied van used to test vehicle collisions without another player.

**Livery**:
The van's paint scheme, lettering and decorative graphics.

**Recovery ghosting**:
The temporary translucent state of a recovered van that lets other vans pass through it while it remains solid against the track and scenery.
